Platform Baseline internal

Addresses and hardware boundaries used by the first smoke test.

Confirmed baseline

The target CPU is the MC6803, the video device is the MC6847, and the software-oriented screen buffer is `$4000-$41FF`. The first executable is placed at `$5000`.

XRoar's MCX128 model exposes bank and map registers at `$BF00` and `$BF01`; the smoke test verifies the all-RAM mapping at `$8000` and restores the default map.

Unverified

Physical MCX-128 reset behavior, bank ownership during cassette loading, alpha-mode values, keyboard scan details, and frame timing remain open. Emulator behavior is not physical-hardware acceptance.

Detailed source inventory is in docs/research.md.
